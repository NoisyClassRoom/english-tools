Click a line, a table cell or a marked word to hide or show it. “Marked parts” are the words that are bold, underlined or white in your Word files (usually the answers). “▶ Board” opens one exercise full screen for the projector (keys: ← → next exercise, W marked parts, H hide lines, S show lines, Esc close).

Edit mode Click in any text to change it. Select words and press “Mark” to make them a hidden answer (or “Unmark”). Enter = new line, Tab / Shift+Tab = indent, Alt+↑↓ = move, the small buttons at the end of a line do the same. Everything is saved automatically.
