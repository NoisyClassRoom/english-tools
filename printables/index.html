🖨 Printables

← Today

Sheets you print for the lesson. Both pages ask you to sign in with the teacher account.

📝Tests A test from the exercise banks of a unit (or several units), in groups A and B.
  • translate, match, circle the answer, complete the sentence
  • answer key and grade table on the sheet
  • QR code so pupils can practise the same unit
 🧩Puzzles Word search, crossword and word scramble made from the words of a unit.
  • word list in English or in Slovenian
  • answer key on a separate page
  • QR code to play it online
 🔺Tarsia A jigsaw of triangles: matching pairs on the touching sides, to cut out and put together.
  • triangle (16 pieces) or hexagon (24 pieces)
  • English ↔ Slovenian words, or short sentences with a gap
  • answer page with the numbered pairs
 🧠Mind maps A map with the topic in the middle and branches with words, for the board or to fill in.
  • ready-made maps, or from the words of a unit
  • radial or tree; with the words or empty boxes
  • edit the text, then print

The pages hold no pupil data. Only the teacher account gets the unit lists and words.
